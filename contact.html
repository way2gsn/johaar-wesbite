Common Questions

Got Questions?

How long does a project typically take? +
Website projects typically take 2–4 weeks. Shopify stores 3–5 weeks. AI ad campaigns can be delivered in as little as 5–7 days depending on scope. WhatsApp bots: 1–2 weeks.
Do you work with clients outside Raipur? +
Absolutely. We work with brands across India and internationally. All collaboration happens remotely — calls, shared documents, and regular check-ins keep everything aligned.
What information do you need to start? +
A brief about your brand, goals, target audience, and any references you love. We'll guide you through a detailed discovery session after our initial call.
Do you offer revisions? +
Yes — all projects include structured revision rounds. We work iteratively and transparently, so you're never surprised at delivery. Major changes beyond agreed scope are quoted separately.
How does payment work? +
We typically take 50% upfront and 50% on delivery. For larger projects, a milestone-based payment plan can be arranged. We accept bank transfer and UPI.
